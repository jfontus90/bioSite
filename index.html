About Me

Ifreke Okpokowuruk is a senior software engineer whose journey reflects both discipline and creativity. He began his academic path at Duke University, where he earned a Division I scholarship, demonstrating not only his athletic ability but also his commitment to excellence. He later continued his education at Morehouse University, where he obtained a degree in computer science.

Beyond academics, Ifreke is passionate about music and is an accomplished piano player. He also enjoys learning and has a strong academic background, including success in spelling bees at an early age.

Professionally, Ifreke has worked with Google and currently works as an engineer at Credit Karma, where he continues to grow his technical expertise and contribute to impactful projects.

He is also a dedicated mentor who enjoys helping others learn, stay motivated, and succeed in their academic and professional journeys.
